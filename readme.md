# 🏢 Business Day Challenge

> **An interactive Business English learning experience focused on workplace vocabulary, daily tasks, professional expressions, and real-world office situations.**

**Business Day Challenge** is an interactive educational website developed as part of a **Business English seminar**. The project transforms a traditional classroom presentation into an interactive learning experience, combining English lessons with quizzes, challenges, XP, levels, badges, and a classroom competition mode.

The goal is simple:

> **Learn. Practice. Challenge yourself. Survive the workday.**

---

## 📚 About the Project

The project was designed to simulate a professional office environment where students can learn and practice common **Business English** vocabulary and expressions.

Instead of presenting the content only through traditional slides, the website turns the seminar into an interactive experience where participants can:

* Learn workplace vocabulary
* Explore different professional roles
* Practice common daily tasks
* Study useful business expressions
* Review basic grammar
* Answer interactive questions
* Earn XP and unlock badges
* Track their performance
* Compete in Classroom Mode

The website was created with classroom presentation in mind, making it suitable for both individual interaction and group activities.

## 🕹️ Main Features

### 📖 Learn

A dedicated learning section containing:

* **Jobs**
* **Daily Tasks**
* **Useful Expressions**
* **Grammar**

Students can explore professional roles and learn how common workplace activities are described in English.

Each section has its own **🌐 EN | PT** switch to translate the sentences into Portuguese and back.

---

### 🎯 Business Day Challenge

The main interactive activity of the project.

The challenge is divided into four phases:

#### Phase 1 — Daily Tasks

Practice vocabulary related to common activities performed during the workday.

#### Phase 2 — Who Are You?

Learn about different professional roles and identify what people do in their jobs.

#### Phase 3 — A Working Day

Follow workplace situations and answer questions about professional routines.

#### Phase 4 — What Would You Do?

Apply what you have learned to practical workplace situations.

During the questions, the **🌐** button translates the answer options into Portuguese. The welcome screen and the Challenge intro also have an **EN | PT** switch.

---

## 🏆 Gamification System

To make the learning experience more engaging, the project includes several game-inspired mechanics.

### XP & Levels

Correct answers reward the player with XP and contribute to their level progression.

### Combos

Answering questions correctly in sequence allows players to build combos.

### Badges

Players can unlock achievements such as:

* 🥇 First Correct Answer
* 🔥 Combo x3
* 📍 Phase Complete
* 🏆 Challenge Survivor
* 💎 Perfect Accuracy

### Ranks

At the end of the challenge, the player receives a rank based on their accuracy (90%+ = CEO Material, 70%+ = Great Professional, 50%+ = Good Start):

| Rank                       | Description                                                                |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 👔 **CEO Material**        | You lead with confidence — the whole office runs better because of you.    |
| ⭐ **Great Professional**   | Reliable, sharp, and ready for whatever the workday brings.                |
| 📈 **Good Start**          | You know the basics — with a little more practice, you will run the place. |
| ☕ **You Need More Coffee** | Tough day at the office. Review the Learn section and try again!           |

## 🏫 Classroom Mode

The project also includes a **Classroom Mode** designed specifically for the seminar presentation.

The class can be divided into:

* **TEAM A**
* **TEAM B**

The presenter can control the questions and keep track of the teams' scores during the activity:

* **15 selected questions** (shuffled every game, including the answer options)
* **Reveal answer** button, showing the correct option and an explanation before awarding points
* Award the point to **Team A**, **Team B**, or **nobody** — and **undo** if you click the wrong team
* **🌐 EN | PT** switch to show the answer options in Portuguese
* Keyboard shortcuts: `Space`/`R` reveal · `1` Team A · `2` Team B · `0` nobody · `U` undo · `N`/`→` next · `T` translate

The objective is to turn the final part of the seminar into a collaborative competition instead of simply ending with a traditional presentation.
